The first public release of Tracer: press a shortcut, select part of your screen, and ask ChatGPT about it, in your own language.

## Download

**[Tracer-Setup-win32-x64.exe](https://github.com/enobot/Tracer/releases/download/v1.0.0/Tracer-Setup-win32-x64.exe)** for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). No other software is needed.

Tracer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. To check the download, compare `Get-FileHash .\Tracer-Setup-win32-x64.exe` in PowerShell with `SHA256SUMS.txt` below.

Install, first-run, and troubleshooting steps: [docs/INSTALL.md](https://github.com/enobot/Tracer/blob/v1.0.0/docs/INSTALL.md).

## What Tracer does

- **Capture with a shortcut.** `Ctrl+Shift+Space`, then drag over an area or right-click for the whole monitor.
- **Answers from your own ChatGPT plan.** Sign in with ChatGPT in your browser; Tracer never sees your password or needs an API key. Answers appear as they are written, and you can ask follow-up questions.
- **Your language.** Choose from 217 languages for answers; the app itself is translated into 20 languages besides English, including Vietnamese, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, and Arabic (right to left).
- **Gemini and Claude.** Copies the screenshot and question and opens the website for you to paste.
- **Prompt library.** Past, favorite, and saved prompts, which you can hide.
- **Runs in the tray.** Closing the window keeps the shortcut working; right-click the tray icon and choose Quit Tracer to exit.

## Privacy

No analytics, no crash reporting, no update checks, and no Tracer server. Screenshots stay in memory and go only to the service you choose, only when you send them. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/enobot/Tracer/blob/v1.0.0/PRIVACY.md).

## Known limitations

- Windows only; macOS and Linux are planned.
- Not code-signed and no automatic updates yet. Check this page for new versions.
- Tested on one monitor at 100% scaling. Reports from multi-monitor and high-DPI setups are welcome.
- The interface translations were AI-generated and have not been reviewed by native speakers yet.
